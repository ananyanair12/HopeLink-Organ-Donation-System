Why It Matters

A Crisis Hiding in Plain Sight

India's deceased organ donation rate has remained below one donor per million population for over a decade. Thousands of patients lose their lives every year while waiting for a suitable organ.

HopeLink is a centralised, accessible platform that connects willing donors with recipients and medical institutions — cutting through the noise of misinformation, logistical barriers, and bureaucracy.

  • ♥ Intelligent organ-compatibility matching
  • 📍 State-prioritised donor discovery
  • 🔒 Secure, private data handling
  • 📋 Step-by-step process guidance
